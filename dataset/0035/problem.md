# Problem 35

For every $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists an integer $N$ such that for every finite simple graph $G$ with $n \ge N$ vertices and minimum degree at least $(1/2 + \varepsilon)n$, and for every set $\mathcal{F}$ of pairs of adjacent edges in $G$ (an incompatibility system) satisfying the property that for every vertex $v$ and edge $uv$ incident to $v$, there are at most $n/8$ pairs $\{uv, vw\} \in \mathcal{F}$, there exists a Hamilton cycle in $G$ such that no pair of adjacent edges in the cycle belongs to $\mathcal{F}$.
