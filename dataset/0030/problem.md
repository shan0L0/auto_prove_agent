# Problem 30

Let $R$ be a commutative unital ring in which $2$ is not invertible. Let $G$ be a finite group whose order divides $64$, and let $H$ be an arbitrary group. If the group algebras $R[G]$ and $R[H]$ are isomorphic as $R$-algebras, then $G$ and $H$ are isomorphic as groups.
