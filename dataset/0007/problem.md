# Problem 7

For any integer $n \ge 1$ and any matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ such that $|A|e = ne$, where $|A|$ is the matrix of entrywise absolute values and $e \in \mathbb{R}^n$ is the all-ones vector, there exists a nonzero vector $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ such that $|Ax| \ge |x|$ entrywise.
