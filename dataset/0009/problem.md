# Problem 9

Let $I_n$ be the set of involutions (elements of order 2) in the symmetric group $S_n$. As $n \to \infty$, the ratio $\frac{|\{(x, y) \in I_n \times S_n \mid \langle x, y \rangle = S_n\}|}{|I_n| |S_n|}$ tends to $3/4$.
