# Problem 20

For all $\gamma > 0$ and $\Delta \in \mathbb{N}$, there is some integer $n_0$ such that for all $n \geq n_0$, every oriented graph on $n$ vertices with minimum semidegree at least $(3/8 + \gamma)n$ contains a copy of every oriented tree on $n$ vertices with maximum degree at most $\Delta$.
