# Problem 24

For $2 \le k \le t < s$, let the Erdős-Rogers function $f^{(k)}_{t,s}(N)$ denote the largest integer $m$ such that every $K^{(k)}_s$-free $k$-uniform hypergraph on $N$ vertices contains a $K^{(k)}_t$-free induced subgraph on $m$ vertices. Then for every $k \ge 4$ and $s \ge k+7$, $f^{(k)}_{k+1,s}(N) = (\log_{(k-2)}N)^{\Theta(1)}$ as $N \to \infty$, where $\log_{(i)}$ denotes the $i$-fold iterated logarithm.
