# Problem 2

Let V be a finite-dimensional real vector space, let m be a positive integer, and let {(A_i, B_i)}_{i=1}^m be pairs of subspaces of V such that dim(A_i \cap B_i) = 0 for every i and dim(A_i \cap B_j) > 0 whenever 1 \le i < j \le m. If a_i = dim(A_i) and b_i = dim(B_i), then
\[
\sum_{i=1}^m \frac{1}{(a_i+b_i+1)\binom{a_i+b_i}{a_i}} \le 1.
\]
