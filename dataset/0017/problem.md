# Problem 17

Let $R_h(k; \ell)$ be the smallest integer $n$ such that every edge coloring of the complete graph $K_n$ with $\ell$ colors contains a monochromatic $K_k$ minor. Then $\lim_{k \to \infty} \frac{R_h(k; 2)}{k \sqrt{\log_2 k}} = 1$.
