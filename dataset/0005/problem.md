# Problem 5

For each integer h ≥ 4, let α_h be the unique positive real root of the polynomial 3^(h-2) x^(h-1) + x - 1. Then for every real number α > α_h, there exists a positive integer M_h(α) such that for every odd integer n > M_h(α), every finite abelian group G of order n, and every subset A ⊆ G with |A| ≥ α n, the restricted h-fold sumset h^∧A := {a1 + ··· + ah | a1, ..., ah ∈ A are pairwise distinct} is equal to G.
