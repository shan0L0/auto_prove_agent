# Problem 8

Let $n, k, t$ be integers such that $k > t \ge 46$ and $n \ge \frac{\sqrt{4t+9}-1}{2}k$. Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a family of $k$-element subsets of $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$. If $|F_1 \cap F_2 \cap F_3| \ge t$ for all $F_1, F_2, F_3 \in \mathcal{F}$, then $|\mathcal{F}| \le \binom{n-t}{k-t}$.
