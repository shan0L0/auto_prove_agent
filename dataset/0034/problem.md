# Problem 34

For any integer $m > 1$, the series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \left( \frac{n^{2m}}{\cosh(\pi n)-1} - \frac{(2^{2m+1}-(-1)^{m(m+1)/2}2^{m+1}+4) n^{2m}}{\cosh(2\pi n)-1} + \frac{2^{2m+2}n^{2m}}{\cosh(4\pi n)-1} \right)$ evaluates to $0$.
