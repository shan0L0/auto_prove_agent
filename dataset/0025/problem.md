# Problem 25

Let $G$ be a finite group. A subgroup $H$ of $G$ is said to be $SS$-quasinormal in $G$ if there exists a subgroup $B$ of $G$ such that $G = HB$ and $H$ permutes with every Sylow subgroup of $B$. If $G$ possesses a maximal subgroup series $G = G_0 > G_1 > \dots > G_n = 1$ (where each $G_i$ is a maximal subgroup of $G_{i-1}$ for $i = 1, \dots, n$) such that $G_i$ is $SS$-quasinormal in $G$ for every $i$, then $G$ is solvable.
