# Problem 26

Let $n \in \{7, 8\}$. There do not exist $x, y \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ such that $x y \neq 0$, $x^n + y^n = 1$, $[\mathbb{Q}(x, y) : \mathbb{Q}] = 4$, and the Galois group of the normal closure of $\mathbb{Q}(x,y)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ is isomorphic to the alternating group $A_4$.
