# Problem 14

Let $G$ be a finite simple graph with at least one vertex. Suppose that every induced cycle in $G$ of length at least 4 has length exactly 4, and that $G$ has no induced subgraph isomorphic to the disjoint union of a single vertex ($K_1$) and a triangle ($K_3$). Then the chromatic number of $G$ is at most $2\omega(G)-1$, where $\omega(G)$ is the clique number of $G$.
