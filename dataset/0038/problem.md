# Problem 38

For each integer $m \ge 2$, there are only finitely many disjoint covering systems (finite sets of congruences $x \equiv a_i \pmod{n_i}$ that exactly partition the integers) such that the minimum modulus is at least $3$, and the multiset of moduli contains exactly one repeated modulus, which is the largest modulus and appears exactly $m$ times.
