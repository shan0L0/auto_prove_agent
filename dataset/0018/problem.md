# Problem 18

There exists an integer $N$ such that for all $n \ge N$, if $S_n$ is the set of all connected spanning subgraphs of the complete graph on $n$ vertices, then for any two distinct edges $e_1, e_2$ of the complete graph, the fraction of graphs in $S_n$ containing both $e_1$ and $e_2$ is at most the product of the fraction of graphs in $S_n$ containing $e_1$ and the fraction of graphs in $S_n$ containing $e_2$.
